Range Installation

Free-Standing · Slide-In · Gas · Electric · Dual Fuel · Pro Range
Pro Note

Gas ranges connect to your home's existing gas line. We connect, leak-test, and verify operation. If you have LP/propane, conversion is NOT included by default — see below.

What Must Be Ready
REQ · 01
Gas Shutoff Valve
Accessible gas shutoff valve within 4 ft of the range location. Valve must operate freely. ½" gas connector standard.
REQ · 02
120V Outlet
Standard grounded 120V outlet for the oven controls, clock, and igniters. Must be reachable from the back of the range.
REQ · 03
Cabinet Opening
Standard 30" wide opening (some pro ranges go up to 36" or 48"). Confirm width and depth match your unit specs.
REQ · 04
Anti-Tip Bracket
Required by code. Comes with the range — installer secures to floor or back wall during install.
!
LP / Propane Conversion is NOT included. If your home runs on propane instead of natural gas, the range needs to be converted with a manufacturer LP conversion kit. The kit is sold separately — we can provide the kit and perform the conversion as an add-on service. Confirm with your sales associate before install day so it's ready.
✓Always Included
  • ✓Disconnect and haul away old range
  • ✓Connect to existing gas line
  • ✓Gas leak test on all connections
  • ✓Connect 120V power
  • ✓Install anti-tip bracket
  • ✓Level range, function test all burners and oven
  • ✓Customer walkthrough
✗Outside Standard Scope
  • ⚠LP / Propane Conversion (kit + labor — available as add-on)
  • ✗New gas line installation or rerouting
  • ✗New electrical outlet
  • ✗Cabinet modification
Pro Note

Electric ranges (smooth top, coil, or induction) require a 240V dedicated circuit. If your home doesn't have one, our team can install electrical work as an add-on identified during measure.

What Must Be Ready
REQ · 01
240V / 40-50A Outlet
4-prong NEMA 14-50 outlet preferred. Older 3-prong outlets require a cord adapter (sold separately).
REQ · 02
Cabinet Opening
Standard 30" wide opening for most. Pro ranges may need 36"–48". Confirm against unit specs.
REQ · 03
Anti-Tip Bracket
Required by code. Comes with the range — installer secures to floor or back wall.
NOTE · 04
Induction Power Draw
Induction ranges may draw more amperage than older electric ranges. Confirm your panel can support the load.
✓Always Included
  • ✓Disconnect and haul away old range
  • ✓Connect to existing 240V outlet
  • ✓Install anti-tip bracket
  • ✓Level and full function test
  • ✓Customer walkthrough
✗Outside Standard Scope
  • ✗New electrical circuit (electrical add-ons quoted at measure)
  • ✗Panel upgrades
  • ✗Cabinet modification
i
Dual fuel ranges use gas burners on top and an electric oven below — combining both worlds. They require both a gas connection AND a 240V electrical circuit.
What Must Be Ready
REQ · 01
Gas Shutoff Valve
Accessible gas shutoff within 4 ft. ½" gas connector standard.
REQ · 02
240V / 30-50A Circuit
Dedicated electrical circuit for the oven. Confirm amperage with product documentation.
REQ · 03
Cabinet Opening
Standard 30" or 36" wide. Pro dual fuel ranges may require larger openings.
!
LP / Propane Conversion not included. If converting to propane, the LP conversion kit must be purchased separately — we can provide it and perform the conversion as an add-on. Mention to your sales associate.
!
Pro ranges (36"–48") require a measure visit. Sizes vary widely between brands and the cutout, hood clearance, and utilities all need verification. Pro ranges over 36" require 2-installer crews and additional labor charges.
What Must Be Ready
REQ · 01
Gas + Electrical
Gas line within 4 ft (for gas/dual fuel models) and 240V circuit for electric oven (dual fuel) or 120V for igniters (gas).
REQ · 02
36"–48" Cabinet Opening
Confirm width with manufacturer specs. Cabinet sides must be heat-rated where required.
REQ · 03
Hood Clearance
Pro ranges generate more BTUs — confirm hood is sized appropriately and at the correct clearance height.
NOTE · 04
Delivery Access
Pro ranges are heavy (300–500 lbs). Doorways must be 36"+ and stairways need to be communicated in advance.
!
LP / Propane Conversion not included. Pro ranges almost always need conversion if running on propane. The LP conversion kit is a separate purchase — we can provide it and perform the conversion as an add-on. Confirm with your sales associate.

Cooktop Installation

Gas · Electric · Induction · Downdraft
Pro Note

Home Depot's program covers a like-for-like swap of an existing gas cooktop. Cutout modifications may be needed even with same-size units. New gas line runs are outside scope.

What Must Be Ready
REQ · 01
Existing Gas Supply
An accessible, working gas shutoff valve within 6 ft of the cooktop location. Valve must operate freely.
REQ · 02
Electrical Outlet
120V grounded outlet within reach for ignition. Required even on gas models.
REQ · 03
Cutout Dimensions
Confirm your countertop cutout matches the new unit's specs. Same brand ≠ same cutout. Provide measurements before install day.
NOTE · 04
Countertop Material
Cutout adjustments on stone, granite, or tile countertops can be performed by our team at an additional fee — to be discussed and approved with your Home Depot store prior to the appointment.
✓Always Included
  • ✓Disconnect and haul away old cooktop
  • ✓Connect to existing gas supply line
  • ✓Gas leak test after connection
  • ✓Set and secure cooktop in cutout
  • ✓Function test all burners
  • ✓Customer walkthrough
✗Outside Standard Scope
  • ✗New gas line installation or rerouting
  • ✗LP / propane conversion
  • ✗Cabinet structural modifications
Pro Note

Electric and induction cooktops require a dedicated 240V circuit. If your home does not have the required circuit, our team can provide electrical work as an add-on service — identified and confirmed during your measure appointment.

What Must Be Ready
REQ · 01
240V Dedicated Circuit
40–50 amp dedicated breaker with correct wire gauge. If not in place, our team can provide as an add-on — quoted at the measure appointment.
REQ · 02
Cutout Match
Verify cutout dimensions against new unit specs. Electric cooktops vary widely between brands.
NOTE · 03
Induction Cooktops
Induction units may draw more amperage than older electric cooktops. Confirm your panel can support the load.
✓Always Included
  • ✓Disconnect and haul away old unit
  • ✓Connect to existing 240V circuit
  • ✓Set and secure in cutout
  • ✓Full function test
  • ✓Customer walkthrough
✗Outside Standard Scope
  • ✗Panel upgrades (electrical add-ons quoted at measure)
  • ✗Countertop cutout fabrication (available at fee)
!
Measurement required before ordering. Downdraft cooktops require precise under-counter clearance for the pop-up duct and blower. Do not order without a pre-install site visit.
What Must Be Ready
REQ · 01
Under-Cabinet Clearance
Minimum 12" of clear space below the counter for duct and blower assembly. Cabinet must be free of obstructions.
REQ · 02
Existing Ductwork
Duct must already be routed to an exterior wall. New duct runs are not included.
REQ · 03
Power
Both cooktop (240V) and blower (120V) circuits must be present and accessible.

Wall Oven Installation

Single · Double · Combination Oven + Microwave
Pro Note

Standard scope covers replacing an existing wall oven of the same type. Match your existing cutout dimensions before ordering. Electrical add-ons can be quoted at measure.

What Must Be Ready
REQ · 01
Cabinet Cutout
Cutout must match new unit width, height, and depth. Measure existing cutout — not the old appliance — and compare to new unit specs.
REQ · 02
240V Dedicated Circuit
30–50 amp dedicated breaker with junction box accessible behind or beneath the oven cavity. If not in place, our team can provide — identified during the measure appointment.
REQ · 03
Access Path
Clear path from entry to kitchen. Wall ovens are heavy — minimum 32" doorways and no stairs without prior notice.
NOTE · 04
Cabinet Strength
Cabinet must be structurally sound and fastened to wall studs. Weak or modified cabinets must be reinforced before install.
✓Always Included
  • ✓Remove and haul away old oven
  • ✓Slide in and secure new unit to cabinet
  • ✓Connect to existing 240V circuit
  • ✓Function test (bake, broil, convection)
  • ✓Customer walkthrough
✗Outside Standard Scope
  • ✗Cabinet cutout modifications
  • ✗Panel upgrades (electrical add-ons quoted at measure)
  • ✗Wood filler or finish cabinetry (color matching)
  • ✗First-time wall oven installation (no existing)
  • ⚠Platforms and metal trim kits — available at additional charge
!
Double ovens are heavier and taller. Confirm your cabinet cutout accommodates the full height of a double unit (typically 50"–52"). Two installers will be sent for double oven installs.
What Must Be Ready
REQ · 01
Extended Cutout Height
Double ovens require taller cabinet openings than single. Measure carefully — typical range is 50" to 52". Confirm with product specs before ordering.
REQ · 02
240V / 50A Circuit
Double ovens typically require a 50A circuit. Electrical add-on available at measure if needed.
REQ · 03
Clear Access
Doorways of at least 36" wide preferred. Stairways must be communicated in advance so a 3-person crew can be arranged.
i
Combination oven + microwave units require a single cutout sized for the combined appliance. Confirm you are replacing an existing combo unit — converting from separate appliances requires cabinet work not included in standard scope.
What Must Be Ready
REQ · 01
Combo Cutout Size
Cutout for combo units is taller than a single oven. Verify height, width, and depth against new unit specs.
REQ · 02
Dual Power Requirements
Some combo units require both a 240V circuit (oven) and a 120V outlet (microwave). Confirm power requirements in product documentation.

Dishwasher Installation

Built-in undercounter · 24" standard opening
Pro Note

Standard scope is replacing an existing dishwasher. First-time dishwasher installs require new plumbing rough-in (water supply, drain line, and electrical) — this must be completed by a plumber before our installer arrives.

What Must Be Ready
REQ · 01
Water Supply Line
Hot water supply valve under the sink, accessible and functioning. ⅜" compression fitting recommended.
REQ · 02
Drain Connection
Drain hose must connect to garbage disposal or sink drain via high-loop. California requires an air gap — if a hole needs to be drilled in the countertop for it, we can do this on any countertop material. Oregon does not require an air gap.
REQ · 03
Dedicated 120V Circuit
15–20 amp dedicated circuit with junction box located under the sink or in a nearby cabinet — not behind the dishwasher. Must be accessible before installation begins.
REQ · 04
Standard Opening
24" wide × 34" high opening standard. European dishwashers may vary. Measure before ordering.
NOTE · 05
Flooring Clearance
New flooring or tile raising the floor height may prevent dishwasher from fitting under countertop. Check clearance before install.
✓Always Included
  • ✓Disconnect and haul away old dishwasher
  • ✓Connect water supply and drain
  • ✓Connect to existing electrical circuit
  • ✓Level and secure dishwasher to cabinet
  • ✓Run test cycle and check for leaks
  • ✓Customer walkthrough
✗Outside Standard Scope
  • ✗First-time install (new plumbing/electrical)
  • ✗New water supply or drain line rough-in
  • ✗New electrical circuit
  • ✗Air gap replacement or new hole for air gap (hole drilling included — see Drain Connection note above)
  • ✗Flooring or cabinet modifications

Over-the-Range Microwave

Replaces OTR microwave or range hood over standard range
Pro Note

OTR microwaves require upper cabinets above the range and a 120V outlet inside the cabinet. If no upper cabinet exists or the outlet is missing, our installer cannot complete the install.

What Must Be Ready
REQ · 01
Upper Cabinet
A full upper cabinet (not just trim) must be present directly above the range for mounting. Cabinet must be secured to wall studs.
REQ · 02
120V Outlet Inside Cabinet
A grounded 120V / 15–20A outlet inside or immediately above the upper cabinet. This is the most common reason installs cannot proceed.
REQ · 03
Vent Configuration
Decide between ducted (exterior vent through cabinet/wall) or recirculating (charcoal filter, no duct). Ducted requires existing ductwork.
REQ · 04
Width Match
OTR microwave should match the width of the range below (typically 30"). Confirm unit width before ordering.
!
No upper cabinet = no install. If no upper cabinet is present, the microwave cannot be safely mounted. A carpenter must install cabinetry before we can proceed.
✓Always Included
  • ✓Remove and haul away old OTR or hood
  • ✓Mount microwave to upper cabinet and wall
  • ✓Connect to existing 120V outlet
  • ✓Configure vent (ducted or recirculating)
  • ✓Function test all features
✗Outside Standard Scope
  • ✗New upper cabinet installation
  • ✗New electrical outlet inside cabinet
  • ✗New ductwork or exterior wall penetration
  • ✗First-time install over no prior unit

Range Hood Installation

Wall-Mounted · Under-Cabinet · Island · Insert (Power Pack)
Pro Note

Wall-mounted hoods require existing ductwork to the exterior and a 120V outlet at the hood location. Duct runs through walls or ceilings are outside scope.

What Must Be Ready
REQ · 01
Existing Exterior Duct
Ductwork must already be installed and routed to the exterior. Duct diameter must match the hood's exhaust port (typically 6" or 8" round).
REQ · 02
120V Outlet
Grounded outlet at or above the hood location, within reach of the power cord.
REQ · 03
Wall Studs
Hood must be anchored into wall studs. Drywall-only mounting is not acceptable for safety.
REQ · 04
Height Clearance
Minimum 24"–30" between cooktop surface and hood bottom. Confirm manufacturer's recommendation.
✓Always Included
  • ✓Remove old hood or OTR
  • ✓Mount hood to wall studs
  • ✓Connect to existing ductwork
  • ✓Connect to existing 120V outlet
  • ✓Function and airflow test
✗Outside Standard Scope
  • ✗New duct runs through walls or ceiling
  • ✗Exterior wall penetration
  • ✗New electrical outlet installation
  • ✗Drywall patching or painting
What Must Be Ready
REQ · 01
Upper Cabinet
Solid upper cabinet for mounting. Hood attaches to cabinet bottom — cabinet must be properly secured to wall.
REQ · 02
Duct or Recirculating
Ducted version needs existing duct through cabinet. Recirculating (ductless) uses charcoal filter — no duct needed but less effective.
REQ · 03
120V Outlet in Cabinet
Power source inside or directly above upper cabinet for the hood's power cord or hardwired connection.
!
Island hoods require ceiling duct routing. Ductwork must already run through the ceiling to an exterior point. This is a complex install — a site assessment is strongly recommended before ordering.
What Must Be Ready
REQ · 01
Ceiling Ductwork
Existing duct must run through the ceiling and exit through the roof or soffit. Must be sized correctly for the hood's CFM rating.
REQ · 02
Ceiling Mounting Support
Island hoods are heavy. Ceiling joists or blocking must be in place. Installer will confirm structural adequacy on-site.
REQ · 03
Hardwired or Outlet Above
120V power at ceiling location. Hardwired connections by a licensed electrician may be required depending on the unit.
Pro Note

An insert hood is a blower / liner unit that mounts inside an existing custom hood enclosure — typically a decorative wood or metal canopy already built into the kitchen. The visible hood structure is part of the cabinetry; we install the working ventilation unit inside it.

!
The hood enclosure must already be in place. We do not build, fabricate, or modify the decorative hood structure. The enclosure must match the insert dimensions and be properly anchored before our installer arrives.
What Must Be Ready
REQ · 01
Existing Hood Enclosure
Custom hood housing must be in place, dimensioned to match the insert width and depth, and structurally anchored to wall studs or ceiling joists.
REQ · 02
Existing Ductwork
Duct must already be routed from inside the hood enclosure to the exterior. Diameter must match insert specs (typically 6", 8", or 10" round).
REQ · 03
120V Outlet Inside Enclosure
Grounded 120V outlet inside the hood enclosure. Higher-CFM units may require hardwiring — confirm with product documentation.
REQ · 04
Width & Depth Match
Insert width must fit precisely inside the enclosure opening. Standard widths are 28", 34", 40", 46". Verify before ordering.
NOTE · 05
CFM & Make-Up Air
Inserts over 400 CFM may require a make-up air system per local code. Make-up air installation is not included.
✓Always Included
  • ✓Remove old insert (if present)
  • ✓Mount new insert inside the existing enclosure
  • ✓Connect to existing ductwork
  • ✓Connect to existing 120V power
  • ✓Function and airflow test
  • ✓Customer walkthrough
✗Outside Standard Scope
  • ✗Building or modifying the hood enclosure
  • ✗New ductwork through walls or ceiling
  • ✗Exterior wall or roof penetration
  • ✗Make-up air system installation
  • ✗Hardwiring (if not pre-existing) — quoted as electrical add-on

Built-In Refrigerator

Panel-Ready · Counter-Depth · Column Refrigerator / Freezer
!
Pre-install site measurement is required. Built-in refrigerators are precision-fit appliances. Our team must measure the opening, cabinetry, and utilities before the unit is delivered. Do not schedule delivery without a confirmed site visit.
Pro Note

Built-in refrigerators are the most complex residential appliance install. Cabinet panels, custom trim, and tight tolerances mean every job is different. We will send measurements to the store — the sales associate will work with you to confirm fit before ordering.

What Must Be Ready
REQ · 01
Precise Opening
Width, height, and depth must be exact. Built-in units have zero tolerance for mismatched openings. Framing adjustments may be needed.
REQ · 02
Dedicated 120V / 20A
Dedicated circuit with outlet inside the recess. Must not share with other appliances.
REQ · 03
Optional: Water Line
If the unit has an ice maker or water dispenser, a ¼" copper or braided line with shutoff valve is required within 6 ft.
REQ · 04
Delivery Access
Built-in fridges are very large and heavy. Confirm all doorways, hallways, and entry paths are clear. Minimum 36" width required.
NOTE · 05
Cabinet Panels
Panel-ready units require custom panels ordered separately. Panels must be on-site before install day. Installation of panels is included.
NOTE · 06
Ventilation Grille
Built-in fridges require a ventilation grille at the top or bottom. Confirm the opening allows for proper airflow per manufacturer specs.
✓Always Included
  • ✓Remove and haul away old refrigerator
  • ✓Slide in and level new unit
  • ✓Connect water line (if existing)
  • ✓Attach cabinet panels (if on-site)
  • ✓Connect to existing electrical
  • ✓Full function test including ice maker
✗Outside Standard Scope
  • ✗Cabinet framing or structural modifications
  • ✗New water line rough-in
  • ✗New electrical circuit
  • ✗Custom panel fabrication or ordering
  • ✗Flooring protection or repair

Washer & Dryer Installation

Top-Load · Front-Load · Stacked · Gas / Electric
✓
We can install your washer and dryer in the garage — just let us know when scheduling so we can confirm utility access.
What Must Be Ready
REQ · 01
Hot & Cold Water Valves
Two working shutoff valves (hot and cold) with hose bib connections behind or beside the washer. Must be within 4 ft of the machine.
REQ · 02
Drain Standpipe
Standpipe or utility sink for drain hose. Minimum 2" diameter, minimum 18" high, maximum 96" high from floor.
REQ · 03
120V / 20A Outlet
Standard grounded outlet within reach of the power cord. Must be a dedicated or laundry-area circuit.
REQ · 04
Level Floor
Floor must be reasonably level. Installer will adjust leveling legs. Severely uneven floors may require a platform.
✓Always Included
  • ✓Disconnect and haul away old washer
  • ✓Connect hot/cold water hoses
  • ✓Connect drain hose to standpipe
  • ✓Level machine and run test cycle
✗Outside Standard Scope
  • ✗New water supply lines or valves
  • ✗New drain standpipe installation
  • ✗New electrical circuit
  • ✗Platform or pedestal installation
What Must Be Ready
REQ · 01
240V / 30A Outlet
4-prong NEMA 14-30 outlet strongly preferred. Older 3-prong NEMA 10-30 outlets require a cord adapter (not included).
REQ · 02
Existing Exhaust Duct
4" rigid or semi-rigid metal duct to exterior. Flexible vinyl duct is not acceptable — it's a fire hazard.
What Must Be Ready
REQ · 01
Gas Shutoff Valve
Accessible gas shutoff within 6 ft. Must accept a standard ½" gas connector. Valve must operate freely.
REQ · 02
120V Outlet
Standard grounded outlet for drum light and controls.
REQ · 03
Existing Exhaust Duct
4" metal duct to exterior. Gas dryers must be ducted — no exceptions.
!
Gas leak test required. Our installer will test all connections before departure. If a leak is detected at the valve, our installer can perform the valve repair as an out-of-scope add-on service — quoted on-site and requires customer approval before proceeding.
!
LP / Propane Conversion is NOT included. If your home runs on propane, the dryer needs to be converted with a manufacturer LP conversion kit. The kit is sold separately — we can provide it and perform the conversion as an add-on service. Confirm with your sales associate before install day.
i
Stacked units are heavier and require two installers. Both washer and dryer requirements above apply. Confirm ceiling height allows the stacked height of both units.
Additional Requirements
REQ · 01
Stacking Kit Required
Make sure to purchase the stacking kit (sold separately) when working with your Home Depot store. It must match your washer/dryer model exactly and be on-site, unboxed, and ready on install day. Universal kits are not accepted.
REQ · 02
Ceiling Clearance
Stacked pair typically reaches 74"–76". Confirm ceiling height in laundry area accommodates this, including duct connections at top.

NOTE · 04

Built-in microwaves recirculate air — cabinet must allow for air intake and exhaust per manufacturer specs. Solid enclosed cabinets with no airflow cannot be used.
