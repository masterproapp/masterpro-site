What we do

A patio or walkway is only as good as what's underneath it. MasterPro excavates, builds a compacted base, and sets pavers, stone, or concrete so the surface stays level and water runs off the right way.

  • Paver patios
  • Walkways and garden paths
  • Retaining walls and seat walls
  • Concrete pads and slabs
  • Natural stone and other outdoor surfaces

How we do it

Every pavers job follows the same process.

  1. Lay out and dig

    We mark the layout and excavate to the right depth for the project.

  2. Build the base

    We add base material and compact it in layers so the surface won't sink.

  3. Set the surface

    We set pavers, stone, or concrete, cut the edges, and install edge restraint.

  4. Finish and clean up

    We sand the joints, compact the surface, and clean the site.

Questions customers ask

Short answers you can give on the spot.

Do they need a permit?

Some projects do, especially taller retaining walls. Rules vary by city, and MasterPro confirms this before any work starts.

Will the pavers shift or sink?

Shifting almost always comes from a weak base. Ours is excavated, compacted in layers, and held in with edge restraint.

Can they buy the materials here?

Usually, yes. Include the product they like on the referral and we'll confirm quantities before they buy.

How long does a patio take?

It depends on size, slope, and prep. The timeline is on the quote after the site visit.
