What we do

If they're tired of watering, mowing, or patching dead spots, turf fixes all three. MasterPro handles the whole job: pulling out the old lawn, building a compacted base, laying and seaming the turf, and cleaning up. The customer just picks the product.

  • Residential lawns and front yards
  • Pet turf with a base built to drain and rinse clean
  • Putting greens
  • Replacing worn or failed turf
  • Removing existing grass or sod

How we do it

Every turf job follows the same process.

  1. Remove the old lawn

    We cut out existing grass, sod, or worn turf and haul it away.

  2. Build the base

    We grade the area and compact a base so the turf drains and stays flat.

  3. Lay and seam

    We roll out the turf, seam the pieces, and secure the edges.

  4. Infill and clean up

    We spread infill, brush the blades up, and leave the yard clean.

Questions customers ask

Short answers you can give on the spot.

Does turf get hot in the sun?

It can run warmer than natural grass in direct sun. Product choice and infill make a difference, and the installer will go over options during the site visit.

Is it okay for dogs?

Yes. Pet installs use a base and infill meant to drain and rinse clean. Mention pets on the referral so it's planned in.

What happens to the sprinklers?

The installer plans for them during the site visit, including capping or adjusting heads in the turf area.

How long will it last?

It depends on the product and how much traffic the area gets. The manufacturer's turf warranty is a good guide, and MasterPro's workmanship terms are listed on the quote.
