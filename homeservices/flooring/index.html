What we do

A floor that lasts starts with prep. MasterPro checks the subfloor, removes and hauls away the old flooring, and installs LVP, laminate, or hardwood with clean transitions and trim.

  • Luxury vinyl plank (LVP)
  • Laminate
  • Hardwood and engineered wood
  • Tile, where available in your area
  • Removing and disposing of old flooring
  • Baseboard, quarter round, and transitions

How we do it

Every flooring job follows the same process.

  1. Remove the old floor

    We pull up carpet, tile, vinyl, or wood and dispose of it.

  2. Prep the subfloor

    We check the subfloor and flatten or repair what the new floor needs.

  3. Install the floor

    We lay LVP, laminate, or hardwood with the right spacing and layout.

  4. Trim and finish

    We install baseboard, transitions, and quarter round, then clean up.

Questions customers ask

Short answers you can give on the spot.

How much extra flooring should they buy?

About 10% extra covers cuts and waste on a standard layout. Diagonal or herringbone patterns need more.

Does the flooring need to sit in the house first?

Many products need to acclimate before install. Follow the manufacturer's instructions, and the installer will confirm.

Can you go over the existing floor?

Sometimes. It depends on what's there and how flat it is. The installer checks this at the site visit.
