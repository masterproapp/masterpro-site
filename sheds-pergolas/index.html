How we do it

Every sheds & pergolas job follows the same process.

  1. Check the site

    We confirm the location, access, and that the ground or slab will work.

  2. Prep the base

    We build or level the base so the structure sits flat.

  3. Assemble to spec

    We build the kit following the manufacturer's instructions.

  4. Anchor and finish

    We anchor it down, check level and square, and clean up.

Questions customers ask

Short answers you can give on the spot.

Does a shed need a foundation?

Nearly every shed needs a level, solid base. We can build one or check that an existing slab or pad will work.

Do they need a permit?

It depends on size and location. Larger sheds and attached pergolas are the most likely to need one, so the customer should check with their city and HOA.

Can they assemble part of it themselves?

We quote the full assembly. A partly built kit is hard to warranty, so it's best to leave the boxes sealed.

How long will it take?

It depends on the kit. The quote includes a time estimate once we know the product and the site.
