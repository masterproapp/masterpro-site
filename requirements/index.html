01 / Universal

Every install.

These six things apply to every job we run, regardless of the appliance. Have them squared away and we can usually be in and out without a second trip.

/ 01
Adult Home

Someone 18 or older must be on-site for the duration of the install. We can't leave the home unattended mid-job.

/ 02
Clear Path

From the truck to the install location — driveway, walkway, doorways, hallways. 36" of clearance minimum, more for larger units.

/ 03
Working Utilities

Water, gas, and electrical at the install location need to be live and accessible. If shut-offs are old or seized, let us know in advance.

/ 04
Old Unit Ready

The old appliance should be empty, defrosted (if applicable), and accessible. Disconnection and haul-away are included.

/ 05
Parking

Our van needs a legal spot within reasonable walking distance. Permits or guest passes are the homeowner's responsibility.

/ 06
Pets Secured

For their safety and ours. Doors will be open. Tools, hoses, and appliance parts will be on the floor for stretches of time.

Dishwasher
Plumbed
  • Water shut-off valve at the sink — working, leak-free, rust-free, and accessible. We can't use the home's main shutoff for a basic install; if the valve isn't working, replacement is available as an extra.
  • CADedicated 120V outlet required in the cabinet bay.
  • ORDedicated 120V outlet or hardwire in the cabinet bay — either is acceptable.
  • CAAir gap required — we cannot install without one. Must be present and not leaking.
  • ORAir gap optional — not required to complete the install, but recommended.
  • Air gap routing — hose must run from the disposal upward, never downward.
  • Hoses and cord cannot run under the house or under the cabinets — routing through cabinets is fine, as long as it stays visible when the cabinet doors are open.
  • Cabinet opening at least 24" wide, 24" deep, 34" tall, with a level base.

Wall / Under-Cabinet
